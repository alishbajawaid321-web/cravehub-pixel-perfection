Our story

Our story

It started with one grill and one promise.

In 2015, three friends opened a 12-seat counter on Food Street, Lahore with a single rule: nothing leaves the kitchen that we wouldn't proudly serve our own family. That rule turned a queue into a brand.

A decade later CraveHub runs 128 stores in 14 cities, still grinding beef fresh daily and still refusing shortcuts. The tagline was never marketing — every bite genuinely tells a story about the people who made it.

Journey timeline

Ten years, one standard

  • 2015

    First 12-seat counter opens on Food Street, Gulberg III, Lahore.

  • 2017

    Signature Burger launches; five stores open across Punjab.

  • 2019

    Central commissary and cold chain built; Karachi debut.

  • 2021

    CraveHub app launches with live rider tracking.

  • 2023

    50th store, ISO-22000 aligned kitchen certification.

  • 2026

    128 stores, CraveBot AI matcher and CravePoints loyalty.

Quality standards

What we refuse to compromise

Fresh, never frozen beef

Ground in-house twice daily and used within 12 hours.

Daily HACCP checks

Temperature, oil quality and hygiene logs audited in every branch.

Certified halal supply

Every protein supplier audited twice a year, no exceptions.

Oil changed on schedule

Fryer oil replaced by measurement, never by guesswork.
